Who this is for: Skeptical HR managers and TA heads who've been burned by job portals full of fake profiles and dead résumés. They give you five seconds. Every section must answer one question — why JREE over Naukri / LinkedIn / campus drives? The whole page is a sales argument, but it must never feel like one — senior-designer restraint is what reads as credible to this audience. Polish signals "real product"; hype signals "another portal."
Page-wide anti-slop guardrails — do NOT use:

three identical rounded feature cards in a row, glowing pricing tiers, gradient mesh / glassmorphism, emoji icons, stock "recruiter shaking hands," fake dashboard screenshots crammed with chart clutter, "Empower your hiring" / "Unlock top talent" copy, animated counters that spin for drama, neon CTA pills. If a section could be lifted onto any SaaS template, redesign it.
Page system (the thing that makes it feel senior):

One disciplined grid the whole way down; sections vary in rhythm but share margins, type scale, and baseline. Consistency is the craft.
Two weights only. Type carries hierarchy — numbers and headlines get weight, everything else stays calm.
One consistent treatment for any imagery/UI shots (same grade, same crop logic) so nothing reads as stock.
Generous whitespace; hairline rules over boxes wherever possible. Restraint over decoration.
Sentence case throughout. No ALL CAPS except small mono eyebrows.

Section 2 — The problem (read their mind)

Eyebrow: Sound familiar? Headline: Campus hiring is broken.

Treatment — "before / after," not three cards. Locked rules (whole page):

No red — bands, gaps, and gating use neutral + amber, never alarm; locked/gated states stay clean, never "rejected"-coded.
Employers see output, not process — never expose exam internals beyond the 5-layer summary; keep candidate PII blurred until unlock in every sample.
Role-fit / band framed as fit for this role, never a verdict on the person.
Bespoke JREE token system; ignore the old HTML prototype styling.
Keep any placeholder logos/testimonials as clearly-marked slots until real assets land.

Motion: minimal and purposeful — the hero card's role-fit verdict resolving once, the 5-step line drawing in on scroll. Nothing loops, nothing spins. Stillness is what makes a sales page read as trustworthy to a skeptic.
